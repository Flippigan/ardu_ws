Key Rules Affecting Autonomy Design

FM-1: Takeoff & Land
  • Takeoff from H to 30ft AGL
  • Transit to L, land vertically
  • Flagger raises flag = approved
  • +20pts landing, +30pts if autonomous
  • Human re-arm at L is allowed
FM-2: Drop Payloads (Red)
  • Takeoff from L to 30ft AGL
  • Transit to F1 or F2
  • Drop red payloads (min 15ft AGL)
  • x2 multiplier if fully autonomous
  • +20pts bonus if no manual trigger
FM-3: Pickup & Re-drop
  • Transit to WA (yellow) or WM (blue)
  • Land/descend to pick up payloads
  • Transit to F1 or F2, drop again
  • x3 multiplier if from WA + autonomous
  • +50pts bonus if no manual intervention
  • Unlimited cycles from WM
End: Return Home
  • Must land at H vertically before timer
  • -20pts if doesn't land at H in time
  • -20pts each ground contact outside L/WA/WM/H
  • 10 minute total timer
