Each model contains:

  • Blue tarp — thin flat box (0.005m thick), blue PBR material mimicking department-store tarps
  • 4 corner stakes — cylinders, ~1 inch diameter (0.0254m), 12 inches tall (0.3048m), brown wood color, placed at tarp corners
  • Static model — no physics, collision disabled
  • Standard Gazebo model structure: model.config + model.sdf
