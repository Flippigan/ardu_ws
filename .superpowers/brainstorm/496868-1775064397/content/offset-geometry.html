offset_forward

Distance from camera to mechanism along the drone's forward axis (positive = mechanism is ahead of camera)

offset_right

Distance from camera to mechanism along the drone's right axis (positive = mechanism is right of camera)

Units

Metres. The PID servo works in body-frame metres from the tag position estimate.

How the existing offset works

During DESCEND_OFFSET, the PID error becomes: error = tag_position - offset. So the drone centres the mechanism (not the camera) over the tag. Once the error is within offset_tolerance, it transitions to DESCEND_FINAL and descends to land.

Currently both offsets are hardcoded to 0.0 in sim_params.yaml — camera and mechanism are treated as co-located.
