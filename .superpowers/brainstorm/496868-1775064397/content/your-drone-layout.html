What to Measure

With the drone on a flat surface, measure from the center of the PiCam lens to the center of the reload mechanism:

offset_forward Straight back from cam to mechanism along the nose-to-tail line. Will be positive (~0.10-0.15m) because the mechanism is behind the camera.
offset_right Sideways offset. If both are on centerline, this is 0.
offset_down (optional) Vertical distance between cam and mechanism. Only matters if the mechanism is significantly above/below the camera — usually negligible.
